Dear Piyush, Rabbit creates a separate copy for each queue where it needs to publish message.
If there are 1 million messages to fan out to 5 queues, we will need 5x space on rabbit.
As you scale it will slow down rabbit and cluster will need more and more space, memory and computing resources.
Your opinions on setting up kafka being difficult etc are mere opinion.
Rabbit also needs similar setup. The way Kafka handles the fan out use case is not 'hacky' rather smart and efficient.
Kafka maintains one copy of each message (ignoring replicas) which consumers on different consumer groups can consume.
You have an obligation to your 200k subscribers to research well before publishing any content your channel. Thanks.
